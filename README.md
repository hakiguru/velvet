<p align="center"><b>Companion NPCs for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)</b><br>
with their own inventory, skills, survival instinct and an AI brain: built in, or any language model.</p>

<p align="center"><b>18+: the mod contains adult content.</b></p>

---

- **She lives on her own.** Hunger, stamina, ten skills that level up, an inventory that grows with her.
- **She fights like a player.** Weapon charged, critical jumps, shield up against arrows and blows, strikes while a monster reloads, a bow for far ones. She fights only what she can beat, runs from the rest, walls herself in, sneaks past the warden, eats to heal, mines ores and gathers blocks.
- **Eight presets.** Elsie, Ellie, Bia, Slime, Allie, Luna, Galath or a blank white player, each with her own animations, voice and scenes.
- **She knows what things are worth.** Every item has a caste, from junk to legendary; she picks up what matters and throws out the rubbish to make room.
- **You can talk to her.** In chat, through her built-in brain or a language model: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Grok, Mistral, OpenRouter, or a local one with Ollama or LM Studio.
- **You can shape her.** The NPC editor has a small Blockbench inside: bones, colours, poses, your own skins. A debug tool for creative sets her instincts, levels and flags.

## Download
Get the jar from [Releases](https://github.com/hakiguru/velvet/releases) and put it into `mods` together with [GeckoLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/geckolib) 4.7+.

## Documentation
Everything about using the mod is in the **[Wiki](https://github.com/hakiguru/velvet/wiki)** (English and Russian).

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.x
- GeckoLib 4.7+
- Java 21

## Build
```
./gradlew build
```
The jar is written to `build/libs/`.
